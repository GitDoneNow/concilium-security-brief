Welcome to the Concilium Security Risk Brief for Monday, November second. Five minutes of security coverage from the last twenty four hours, to increase your situational awareness without decreasing your productivity. Here's what matters today.

Today: cyclone evacuations in India, another ship struck in the Strait of Hormuz, hacked emergency alert apps in Russia and Ukraine, an arrest in the Moscow threat case, and an attack in northeastern Kenya.

We start in India. Reuters reports authorities began evacuating people along India's eastern coast today, a day before a severe cyclonic storm is expected to make landfall, in a region still recovering from a deadly cyclone last week. Reuters says thousands have been evacuated.

Turning to the Strait of Hormuz. The Institute for the Study of War and the Critical Threats Project report that the U.K. Maritime Trade Operations center said an unidentified projectile struck a vessel in the Strait of Hormuz yesterday. They assess Iran likely carried out the strike as part of its efforts to disrupt civilian shipping.

Next, a cyber item. Radio Free Europe reports hackers targeted emergency alert apps in Russia and Ukraine over the past two days, sending false warnings of nuclear attacks, radiation and chemical threats. In Ukraine, it says the Air Alert app was compromised and sent fake warnings of radiation, street fighting and an incoming Russian attack.

An update on the Moscow threat. Russia's state news agency TASS reports the F S B security service said that, while checking U.S. information about a possible attack, it detained a teenager who had posted on TikTok threatening to commit a crime at Tsaritsyno Park. TRAC first flagged the arrest.

And in Kenya. The Kenyan outlet Kahawa Tungu reports a police officer was shot and injured yesterday in a suspected al Shabaab attack in Bodhai, in Garissa County.

Now a few quick notes by region.

In Europe, an update on southern Ukraine. Reuters reports Ukraine's military said it evacuated one hundred sixty seven people from threatened areas of the Kherson region as a temporary local ceasefire expired. Reuters also reports Russian strikes cut power to thousands in Ukraine, and Radio Free Europe reports Russian authorities said more than one hundred drones were destroyed in an attack on the Nizhny Novgorod region. Separately, the U.S. State Department says protests over housing and the cost of living are taking place across Portugal, including in Lisbon and Porto, and that police have recently used tear gas.

In the Middle East, Reuters reports, citing health officials, that Israeli strikes killed at least three Palestinians in the Gaza Strip. And Radio Free Europe reports President Trump again threatened to resume air strikes against Iran.

In Africa, Nigerian outlets including Vanguard report Zamfara State police confirmed the rescue of three of six Islamic clerics who were abducted by bandits on September twelfth.

And in cyber, Reuters reports temperatures soared in freezers at Swedish grocery stores, and one store blamed hackers.
