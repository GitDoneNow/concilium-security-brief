Welcome to the Concilium Security Risk Brief for Monday, November twenty third. Five minutes of security coverage from the last twenty four hours, to increase your situational awareness without decreasing your productivity. Here's what matters today.

Today: Ukraine says it sank a Russian warship in a Libyan port, troops in northern Nigeria rescue most of a group of abducted children, a deadly Russian strike near Kyiv, Houthi attacks on Saudi infrastructure, and an update on Ebola in Congo.

We start in Libya. Reuters reports President Volodymyr Zelenskiy said Ukraine sank the Russian naval corvette Merkury in a Libyan port using seven naval drones. Radio Free Europe Radio Liberty reports Libya says a suspected sea drone caused an explosion aboard a Russian ship docked at a naval base near Benghazi, injuring ten Russian and Libyan crew members. The Institute for the Study of War says Ukrainian forces struck two Russian vessels near Benghazi, destroying at least one.

Next, Nigeria. Voice of Nigeria reports the Nigerian Army says troops rescued eighteen of twenty two children from the Boys' and Girls' Brigade who were abducted by suspected terrorists during an early morning road walk in the Mother Cat community, in the Kachia and Kajuru area of Kaduna State. The army says the search for the other four continues. The terrorism monitor TRAC also reported the rescue.

Turning to Ukraine. Reuters reports a Russian attack on Dymer, in the Kyiv region, killed eleven people, according to an official. Earlier, Reuters reported President Zelenskiy said at least fourteen people were missing after a Russian drone hit a building in the region.

In Saudi Arabia, the Institute for the Study of War and Critical Threats say the Houthi movement targeted Saudi critical infrastructure on Saturday and Sunday, November twenty first and twenty second, and claimed a strike on Petro Rabigh oil facilities. They describe it as part of a campaign to press Saudi Arabia to end military operations in Yemen.

And an update on the Ebola outbreak in Congo. Reuters reports the head of the World Health Organization says fighting near the outbreak area is disrupting the response. A separate Reuters analysis of satellite imagery shows graveyards in eastern Congo expanding much faster than the official Ebola death count, pointing to a major undercount.

Now a few quick notes by region.

In Europe, an update on Friday, November twentieth: Reuters reports Polish prosecutors say three Russian missiles flew into Poland that day. The Institute for the Study of War says a Russian jet powered drone violated Moldovan airspace twice on Saturday, November twenty first. And in England, Reuters reports thousands of people were evacuated from a concert in Manchester last night after reports that an unknown substance had been released, British police said.

In Africa, the International Committee of the Red Cross says intensifying hostilities in Ethiopia's Tigray region have forced health facilities to suspend services.

In the Pacific, the U.S. Geological Survey reported a preliminary magnitude six point five earthquake in the Vanuatu region.
